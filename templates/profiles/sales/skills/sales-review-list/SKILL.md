# Sales review list

1. Score fit against the stated ICP. Label inferred vs observed.
2. Up to three contact roles, not real people unless the user named them.
3. Draft one email and one short note. Mark both do-not-send.
4. Skip anyone already in an active sequence.
5. Stop. Do not contact anyone.
