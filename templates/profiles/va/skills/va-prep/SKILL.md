# VA prep

1. List what came in. Cite the thread, event, or item number.
2. Mark what needs a human (unclear time, spend, publish, missing amount).
3. Draft replies or slot options. If no other slots are known, write none.
4. End with the exact action waiting on approval, by item number.
5. Stop until the user names which item numbers to execute.
