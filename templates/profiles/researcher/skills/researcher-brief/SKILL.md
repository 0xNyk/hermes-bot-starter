# Researcher brief

1. Restate the question in one line.
2. Read the named files or official docs first. Skip extra fetches unless a citation is missing.
3. Findings with path or URL plus section. Separate observation from inference.
4. Unknowns: say what was not checked.
5. One next step a human can take. Stop. Do not implement.
